# Open Controls

Practical governance templates for AI-assisted delivery, published by Vialian.

**Status:** Draft. Maturity level 0: created and tested by the authors, with no external review yet.

## Contents

| Path | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `templates/AI_WORK_CONTRACT_TEMPLATE.md` | Provider-neutral AI Work Contract. It defines the objective, scope, authority, required evidence and human approval point before an AI agent starts consequential work. | Draft, 16 November 2026 |

## How to use

Fill in the template before execution and keep it with the work. At completion, review the result against the same contract and record every exception with its evidence.

## Limits

These templates do not replace security controls, access management, a model risk management framework or legal review. They do not approve work. A human remains accountable for consequential use.
